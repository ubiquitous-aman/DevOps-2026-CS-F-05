How It Works

From Requirement to Campus Offer

1
Company Submits Drive

Job role, CTC package, bond details, and candidate requirements.

2
TPO Sets Criteria

Branches, min CGPA, 10th/12th % cut-offs, then publishes the drive.

3
Eligible Students Apply

Auto-checked eligibility; instant 1-click verified application.

4
Rounds Clearance

Aptitude, Tech, and HR interview results updated stage by stage.

5
Selection & Offer

Placement confirmed, live student notification, and official statistics update.
